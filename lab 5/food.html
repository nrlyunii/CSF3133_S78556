About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local food stalls for three days of eating, music and culture by the river. Visitors can try famous dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam prepared by local businesses.

The festival also supports local entrepreneurs and students. Cooking demonstrations are held every evening and all stalls follow environmentally friendly practices using reusable and biodegradable packaging.

Free entry for all UMT students. Please bring your matric card.
