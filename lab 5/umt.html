About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu. The university is well known for its research in marine science, fisheries, and aquatic resources.

UMT offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes from all fields such as computer science, technology, marine, counselour, political science, and finance. The university focuses on producing knowledgeable graduates and developing research that contributes to society and the environment.

The campus provides modern facilities, research centres, libraries, laboratories and student activities that support academic excellence and personal development.
